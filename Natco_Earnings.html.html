Natco Pharma (NATCOPHARM)

Global Investment Research | Equity Analysis | Q4 FY26
Investment Summary: Natco Pharma delivered a muted set of results for Q4 FY26 and the full year, primarily dragged down by a steep decline in its core Revlimid (Lenalidomide) revenue contribution. For the full year, consolidated revenue fell by 8.5% YoY to ₹4,375.9 crores. Management provided a sobering outlook for FY27, guiding for a further structural reset in earnings. While the near-term profile looks challenging, a strong net cash position of ₹2,400 crores provides massive balance sheet optionality.

Concall Key Takeaways

Management Tone & Critical Insights

Candid, grounded, and intensely realistic. Management directly addressed the structural cliff in Lenalidomide revenue. They have guided for a major base revision for FY27, targeting revenues of ₹3,400–3,500 crores and PAT of ₹700–750 crores. Profits are being reinvested to transition from a US-reliant model into an ecosystem spread across India, US, Brazil, Canada, and South Africa.

Red Flags & Positive Surprises

Management openly stated that they have zero major first-to-file product launches scheduled for the US market in FY27. Other expenses rose sharply due to an engineering inventory write-down. However, domestic Semaglutide launch is capturing strong market share, generating ₹4–5 crores/month via partner channels, and three key manufacturing plants were cleared by the USFDA.

Investment Thesis Update

Final Stance: HOLD
Conviction: High
Time Horizon: 18–24 Months

Bull Case: A clean balance sheet holding ₹2,400 crores in net cash provides heavy ammunition to execute mid-to-large scale acquisitions outside India. Emerging geographies are performing optimally, with Brazil's oncology market scaling cleanly.

Bear Case: With PAT guided down to a midpoint of ₹725 crores, the stock faces an immediate contraction in underlying earnings capacity. Growth acceleration is heavily back-ended into FY28 and beyond.
